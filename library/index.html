Your exercise library

The Muscle focus list at the top ranks 24 specific muscles (forearms first), grouped upper body, lower body and core. Each has its own Videos list, showing NA until a good video is added. Below it, the nine functional groups hold the full exercise menu.

You don't need to isolate every muscle. Train these nine functional groups, with extra attention to running muscles and joints. Required = in your current weekly plan. Good to have = proven swaps and progressions for when equipment is busy or you want variety.

Every exercise in the weekly plan has OR alternatives in red, tagged ⚙️ Machine 🏋️ Dumbbell 🧍 Bodyweight ➰ Band. Do the main exercise or one swap, never both.

Difficulty is rated for a returning lifter at 45: Easy stable, low-skill, safe to start · Medium needs balance or technique · Hard high skill or load — add only after 8–12 weeks of consistent training. Stop any movement that causes knee pain.

This week's 6-day plan

Tap a day to show only that session's cards below · tap an exercise name to search it on YouTube

Mon · Wed · Fri = gym strength days. Tue · Thu · Sat = run first, then a short light session (bodyweight, band or light dumbbells — home-friendly, nothing heavy for the legs). Sun = rest or an easy walk. Heavy legs sit on Monday so they've recovered by the weekend run — keep Tuesday's run easy, the day after legs. Friday has no leg work before Saturday's run. If fatigue builds, skip the light session after a run first — never add heavy legs to a run day.

Gym days: warm up 5 min (easy bike or brisk walk) and do one light practice set of the first exercise. Getting stronger: when you hit the top of the rep range on every set with 2 reps still in the tank, add a little weight next week. Every 4–6 weeks: take an easy week — about 40% fewer sets and ~10% lighter weights. After every run: 5 min easy walk before the light session.
