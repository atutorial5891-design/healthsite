The plan

Cook one kichdi batch, split it three ways for breakfast/lunch/dinner, add the same daily extras around it. Numbers below use the quantities as given — flag anything that's off and the numbers move with it.

⚠️ Assumptions made to compute this (correct these and the totals change): mixed vegetables ≈100g, tempering uses ~2 tsp oil/ghee for the whole batch, "kala chana" = roasted (dry, crunchy snack) not boiled, fruit portion ≈150g, curd values are generic toned-curd figures — Milky Mist's actual label may differ slightly. Branded items (paneer, curd) are worth checking against the pack label when you buy them.
