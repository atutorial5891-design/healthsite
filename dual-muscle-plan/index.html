Trainer-style split · 24-muscle coverage

Two similar muscles, every day

Each day pairs two complementary targets (e.g. Back + Biceps). Gym days are Mon · Wed · Fri. Tue · Thu · Sat start with a run, then a short light session. Together, the week hits all 24 Exercise Library muscles — extras stay light (~10–15 min) so run days stay recovery-friendly.

Each day card lists the library muscles it covers. The directory at the bottom shows which day trains each one, with YouTube search links.

Week at a glance

Tap a day to focus · All shows everything

Why this order: heavy legs sit on Monday so they recover before the weekend run. Keep Tuesday’s run easy the day after legs. Friday is pull (Back + Biceps) with no heavy leg work before Saturday’s run. Light extras on run days (rotator cuff, neck, shins, hip flexors) fill the remaining library muscles without heavy thigh load. Sun = rest or an easy walk.

Common stretches — every workout day

Same short routine before and after. Do these on gym days and after the run on run days.

🔥

Before — warm-up · ~5 min

  • Easy bike or brisk walk — 2–3 min
  • Arm circles + shoulder rolls — 20 each way
  • Cat–cow or thoracic openers — 8 slow reps
  • Bodyweight squats or hip hinges — 10 easy
  • On gym days: 2–3 light ramp-up sets of the first exercise

After — cool-down · ~5 min

  • Easy walk — 2 min (especially after a run)
  • Chest doorway stretch — 30 s each side
  • Half-kneeling hip-flexor stretch — 30 s each side
  • Seated hamstring reach — 30 s each side
  • Child’s pose or child’s pose + side reach — 45–60 s

Overall fat burn

Home fat-burn finisher

Short lower-belly / home circuit for calorie burn — not spot reduction. Fat loss still comes from your deficit, protein, steps, and the main lift + run plan.

2–3× / week · standard
  • Do 2–3 times per week — enough to raise weekly energy burn without stacking fatigue on top of 3 gym + 3 run days.
  • Best days: Wed (after Chest + Triceps), Thu (after run + light calves/hips), and optionally Sat (only if the run felt easy — keep it short).
  • Skip or keep very short on Mon (heavy legs) and on any day you feel wiped — more than ~4×/week is usually overkill with this schedule.
  • Place it after the main session, then do cool-down stretches. More detail also lives on the Belly Fat Videos page.
