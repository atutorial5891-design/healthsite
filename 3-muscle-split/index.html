Workout Split

3 main muscles + 1 small muscle · every day

Same three pairings twice a week. Abs and forearms rotate as the third slot. The 4th slot (Face & Neck, Wrist & Hand, Feet & Ankle) is placed so it never overlaps a muscle already worked that day. Videos below come from the Exercise Library.

🔥 Monday Chest + Triceps + Abs + Wrist & Hand
💪 Tuesday Back + Biceps + Forearms + Feet & Ankle
🦵 Wednesday Legs + Shoulders + Abs + Wrist & Hand
⚡ Thursday Chest + Triceps + Forearms + Face & Neck
🏆 Friday Back + Biceps + Abs + Feet & Ankle
🏋️ Saturday Legs + Shoulders + Forearms + Face & Neck
😴 Sunday Rest & Recovery

Why the 4th slot sits where it does
  • 🤲 Wrist & Hand · Mon / Wed — never on a Forearm day (Tue, Thu, Sat), and not on Back + Biceps days where heavy pulling already drains grip.
  • 🦶 Feet & Ankle · Tue / Fri — never on a Leg day (Wed, Sat), so calves and ankles get a full day to recover after squats and calf raises.
  • 🙂 Face & Neck · Thu / Sat — never on a Back day (Tue, Fri), because rows and face pulls already load the traps and neck.
  • Each pair is at least 48 h apart, and every gym day gets exactly one small muscle.

How to run this split

  • Each gym day has two primary muscles plus Abs or Forearms as the third.
  • Pick 2–3 exercises per primary muscle from that category’s video list (or the matching cards in the Exercise Library).
  • Keep 1–2 Abs or Forearm finishers short — quality over volume after the main lifts.
  • The 4th slot is a 5–8 minute block: 2–3 light moves, bodyweight or a band. Do it last, or as a warm-up for Wrist & Hand and Feet & Ankle.
  • Face & Neck stays very light: chin tucks and gentle isometric holds. Never load the neck with weight, and stop if you feel dizzy or get pain or tingling.
  • Sunday is full rest. If joints feel beat up mid-week, drop the 4th and third muscles and keep the two primaries.
